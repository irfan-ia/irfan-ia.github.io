Rabta — POS for mobile phone shops

Sell and buy new and used mobile phones and accessories, keep every IMEI on record, and know your stock, cash and khata at a glance — on the shop computer and on your phone.

New & used phones

Sale and purchase of new and second-hand phones, with the IMEI / serial number of every set.

Accessories & stock

Chargers, covers, handsfree and more — stock that updates with every sale and purchase.

Khata & payments

Customer and supplier accounts, credit, cash, bank and Easypaisa / JazzCash payments.

Mobile app

See stock, sales and khata on your phone, and make entries away from the counter.

Reports

Daily sale, profit, stock and cash reports for the owner.

Backup

Backups on the shop computer, a USB drive, email, or your own Google Drive.

Your data stays with you

Rabta runs on the shop's own computer. Your sales, stock and customer records are kept there — not on our servers. Google Drive backup is optional and can only touch the backup files Rabta creates. Read the privacy policy.

  1. Install Rabta on the shop computer.
  2. Add your phones, accessories, customers and suppliers.
  3. Sell and buy at the counter or from the mobile app.
  4. Back up automatically — locally or to your own Google Drive.
