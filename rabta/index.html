FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Does Rabta need internet?

No. The shop works on its own computer without internet. Internet is needed only for Google Drive backup and for using the phone app away from the shop.

Where is my data kept?

On your shop's computer. We do not receive your sales, stock or customer records. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I keep the IMEI of every phone?

Yes — every new and used set is saved with its IMEI / serial number, and you can search by it.

What does Google Drive backup access?

Only the backup files Rabta creates in your own Drive (and the email address of the connected account, shown on the Backup screen). Nothing else in your Google account.
