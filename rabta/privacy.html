Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Rabta software ("Rabta", "we", "us") handles and how. Rabta is point-of-sale software for mobile phone shops (sale and purchase of new and used phones and accessories, stock, khata and accounts) developed by Irfan Maqsood. It is installed and runs on the shop owner's own computer, with an optional mobile app that connects to that computer.

In short: your shop's data stays on your own computer. We do not collect it, sell it or see it. If you turn on Google Drive backup, Rabta can only access the backup files it creates in your own Drive.

1. Data stored by the software

Rabta stores the information you enter — such as products and phones (including IMEI / serial numbers), stock, sales, purchases, customers and suppliers (names, phone numbers, addresses), payments, expenses and user accounts of your staff — in a database on the shop's own computer (the "main computer"). When a shop buys a used phone, the shop may record the seller's details (for example name, phone number and identity card number) as the shop's own record. Other computers in the shop and the mobile app connect to the main computer.

This data is not sent to us or to any server operated by us. The shop owner is in control of it and is responsible for the information they enter about their customers and sellers.

2. Information we receive

  • License: to issue a license key, you give us a "machine code" shown in the software. It is a one-way code made from your computer's ID; it does not contain your business data.
  • Support: if you contact us, or send us an error report from the software, we receive what you choose to send (for example your name, phone number and the report). Error reports describe the technical problem and do not include your sales or khata records. We use this only to help you.

3. Google Drive backup (Google user data)

Google Drive backup is optional and off until the shop owner connects it.

  • What we request: only the Google permission drive.file ("see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app").
  • What it is used for: uploading backup files of your shop database to your own Google Drive, listing those backup files so you can restore one, downloading a backup when you restore, and deleting old Rabta backups to save space. Rabta cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Where the access token is kept: the Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on your shop's main computer only. It is never sent to us.
  • Sharing: we do not receive, store, sell, share or transfer your Google user data to anyone. Backup files travel directly between your computer and Google.
  • No advertising or AI training: Google user data is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve or train any artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Disconnecting: you can disconnect Google Drive from the Backup screen at any time, or remove Rabta's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
Rabta's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Mobile app

The Rabta mobile app connects to the shop's own computer to show and enter shop data. It may keep a copy of some data on the phone so it can work without a connection; that copy stays on the phone and is sent only to the shop's computer. If the phone's camera is used to scan a barcode or QR code, the image is processed on the phone and is not sent to us.

5. Security

Passwords are stored as secure hashes, staff only see what their role allows, important actions are recorded in an activity log, and secrets on the computer are encrypted. Please keep your computer and phones protected with a password and keep regular backups.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your computer for as long as you keep it. Uninstalling Rabta does not delete your database or backups, so you do not lose data by accident; you can delete them yourself. Information you send us for support is deleted when it is no longer needed, or sooner on request.

7. Children

Rabta is business software for shop owners and their staff. It is not meant for children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
