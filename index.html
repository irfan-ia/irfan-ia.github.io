Your data

Your data stays yours

Saudagar runs on your own computer. Your sales, stock and khata are stored in a database on that computer — not on our servers.

  • Automatic daily backup to a folder on your computer
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive
  • Restore your whole shop on a new computer in minutes

How Google Drive backup works

When the shop owner chooses Connect Google Drive, Google asks for permission. Saudagar only gets access to the backup files it creates itself — it cannot see any other file in your Drive. You can disconnect at any time from the Backup screen or from your Google Account.

FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Do I need internet to use Saudagar?

No. The software runs on your own computer inside the shop. Billing, stock and khata work without internet. Internet is only needed for the mobile app away from the shop, WhatsApp and Google Drive backup.

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer, in your shop — not on our servers. Daily backups go to a folder on your computer, and optionally to your own Google Drive.

Is it a one-time payment?

Yes. The shop software is a lifetime license — pay once and use it forever. Only the mobile app has a small monthly fee, and it is optional.

Can my staff use it on their phones?

Yes. You decide who can use the phone and what each person can see and do, and you can turn any phone off at any time — their computer login keeps working.

Does it work in Urdu?

Product and customer names can be in Urdu, bills print Urdu names, and the mobile app understands Urdu voice entry.

Can you make software for my type of business?

Yes. We build custom systems — for schools, academies, factories, real estate, mobile shops, farms and more — on the same proven foundation as Saudagar POS.
