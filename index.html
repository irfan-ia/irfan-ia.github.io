Your data stays yours

Saudagar runs on your own computer. Your sales, stock and khata are stored in a database on that computer — not on our servers.

  • Works without internet at the counter
  • Automatic daily backup to a folder on your computer
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive
  • Restore your whole shop on a new computer in minutes

How Google Drive backup works

When the shop owner chooses Connect Google Drive, Google asks for permission. Saudagar only gets access to the backup files it creates itself — it cannot see any other file in your Drive. You can disconnect at any time from the Backup screen or from your Google Account.
